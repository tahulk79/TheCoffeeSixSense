# Seven café days — pilot chapter (v0.6.0)

A player arrives as a café visitor, rather than being required to run a counter.
Seven authored visits follow Linh (sketching before moving away), Luca (learning
to brew at home), Camila (between jobs), and the owner. Sketch, rainy photograph,
blank note, brewing notebook, postcard and plant connect the scenes.

## Interaction and progression

- Starting a visit randomly assigns one of two narrative-compatible invitations.
  The assignment is saved and stays fixed across reload/language changes. The
  three activity types are short branching conversation, code-drawn table-photo
  composition (two lighting options and three objects), and a voluntary link to
  the existing brew station. This is a small prototype, not seven bespoke games.
- Every invitation is optional. Listen instead, or close the visit directly;
  both advance the same story with no score threshold, stamina, penalty or XP.
- Closing stores the ending and memento. A separate explicit button starts the
  next visit; neither loading the page nor a real calendar day advances it.
  The player can continue immediately or return after any length of absence.
- Conversation replies change a short response, not the overall plot. Recorded
  compositions and replies are visible when revisiting completed scenes.
- The café board opens completed visits without advancing or rewriting them.
  The seventh visit has an explicit chapter ending; no unavailable chapter is
  advertised as ready. Free practice and the daily catalog remain available.
- Brew invitations use the existing keep/replace protection for an active cup.
  Opening an invitation is recorded as `brew-opened`, never as proof of a brewed
  or saved cup, actual sensory learning or story-task success.

## Storage and limits

`c6s.seven-visits.v1` is a separate versioned localStorage envelope. Only a valid,
contiguous sequence of up to seven visits is accepted. The last may be open;
previous visits must be closed. Mode-specific choices and image composition
values are checked. Unknown versions and invalid data are preserved and can be
exported as original bytes. Storage failures leave persisted chapter progress
unchanged and allow retry. Export creates a local JSON backup; there is no import
UI, cloud synchronization, backend, account or cross-device guarantee.

All people/dialogue are fictional. Coffee facts reuse the bounded daily catalog;
country/processing names do not invent verified farm histories or sensory scores.
The chapter does not change pouring physics, existing game targets, real-brew
records, local-day catalog rotation or scientific uncertainty disclosures.
Photo composition is SVG generated by code; it does not capture a camera image,
use AI, upload images or produce a separately downloadable photograph.

## Verification

- 84 automated tests: existing 79 plus five chapter checks covering bilingual
  content/discovery references, seven optional-only visits, random assignment
  persistence, branch/photo records, invalid cross-mode choices and recovery.
- Pages and Vite builds.
- Chromium 320/390/820/1440 px: two full seven-visit playthroughs per width,
  covering both invitation choices for every scene; photo selection/reload,
  conversation branches, brew invitation, VI/EN, archive, finale, export and
  horizontal bounds. Separate listen-only, quota-retry and corrupt-data cases.
- Existing eight-case pouring regression: original control coordinates, focus,
  scroll and station DOM identity remain stable through all three stages.

This is functional/visual verification, not evidence of human enjoyment or return
rates. Safari and actual mobile hardware were not verified. A useful next playtest
asks which guest players remember, whether they voluntarily start a second visit,
and whether they return to learn what happens to that guest.
